## Known Risk

- [Touch Gesture Collisions]: Top-third to bottom-third swipe could accidentally trigger arena aim/firing during gesture motion. Mitigation: Track gesture points and defer aim lock if vertical travel velocity matches intentional swipe.
- [CPU Speed Multiplier Math]: Scaling speed must not result in division by zero or negative timers. Mitigation: Clamp `cpuSpeedMultiplier` strictly between 0.25 and 2.0.
- [Rapid Fire Bullet Exhaustion]: Firing at 80ms interval with 5-bullet cap quickly jams without expanding bullet pool. Mitigation: Expand max player bullets to 12 when rapid fire cheat is active.

## Red Team Findings

- Gesture boundaries: swipe down requires starting y < 285, ending y > 570, |deltaX| <= 160, preventing accidental activation during combat aim.
- Bullet flood guard: rapid fire expands player bullet cap from 5 to 12 without unbounded bullet allocation.
- Difficulty clamp: CPU speed strictly clamped between 0.25x and 2.0x, eliminating NaN or negative velocity risks.
